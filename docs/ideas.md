# Ideas for after the port

The owner's ideas for where OpenPCrash goes once the original game is done. Nothing here is
started. Each one gets its own plan doc when it is picked up.

## More game

- **New maps.** Bigger maps built for more players, beyond the original's arenas.
- **A bigger open-world mode.** Something larger than an arena: roam a city, find fights.
- **New game modes.** For example "Scoobee Survivors": one machine against waves of drones and
  machines that keep getting harder.
- **A mod browser.** Players find, install and switch maps, parts and modes in the game.

## More content from the original's art

- **Study the Scoobees and guns.** Learn how Genki built the models (parts, proportions, poly
  budgets, texture layout), then parametrise them so new parts can be generated in the same style.
- **Content expansions.** More guns, bodies, legs and parts that are low poly by today's
  standards, made from that study as our own models, not copies.
- **Generated companies.** New Scoobee makers with their own lines of parts and their own looks,
  generated offline and then hand-picked.

## Strategy lab

- **Find the exploits and the best builds.** Nobody has worked out Phantom Crash's numbers. With the
  tables, the formulas and pc_sim running fights headless, we can rank weapons by damage per second
  and per NY, find the best build for each budget and day, list the exploits (the tune-99 pistol,
  triple damage from behind, the level-0 AI) and test strategies against the AI in thousands of fights.
  A write-up, and a page on the archive site.

## Crisp art

- **SVG versions** of the HUD, the menus and the in-game 2D art, so they render sharp at any
  resolution. These could be used in the game at high resolutions, on the site and in posts.

## S.L.A.I.

- **OpenSLAI.** A port of S.L.A.I. (the PS2 sequel) the same way, then a combined edition with the
  best of both games in one.
